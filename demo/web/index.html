Where the evidence lives

The spectrum shows how much energy the image carries at each spatial frequency. Generated CIFAKE images carry more fine detail than photographs; resizing and blurring remove it, which is why the detector trained on clean images then calls generated images real. Sharpening adds it back, and photographs start to look generated. Both detectors were trained on CIFAKE, whose generated images come from Stable Diffusion 1.4. They are research artefacts for studying robustness, not general-purpose detectors: on images from other generators they are close to chance. Image handling here uses the browser's own resampling and JPEG encoder, so results can differ slightly from the paper's. Code and weights: cifake-robustness.
